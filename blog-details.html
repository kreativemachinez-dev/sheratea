The Journey of Tea From Garden to Cup

Tea is more than just a drink it’s a story of nature, people and tradition. From the misty hills where it’s grown to the moment it reaches your cup, every step in the journey of tea is filled with care, craftsmanship and a deep respect for the land.

In this article, we take you through the incredible journey of tea leaves, from lush gardens to your favourite cup, and explore what makes each step so special.

1. The Tea Gardens

Our journey begins in the lush tea gardens, where tea plants thrive in fertile soil and a favourable climate. Skilled tea growers carefully nurture the plants, ensuring the leaves are healthy, vibrant and full of flavour. The natural surroundings, fresh air and balanced rainfall all contribute to the unique taste of our teas.
