A Rich Heritage in Every Cup

Shera Tea was born from a simple belief everyone deserves a great cup of tea. What started as a passion for quality tea has grown into a trusted brand, bringing the finest blends from nature to households across India.

With a deep respect for tradition and a focus on innovation, we continue to create blends that inspire moments of joy, togetherness and warmth.

Quality First

Sustainability

Togetherness

We believe tea creates moments that bring people closer.

From Garden to Your Cup

Our teas travel a remarkable journey from lush tea gardens, through careful harvesting, to carefully crafted blends that reach your home. At every step, we ensure quality, freshness and a commitment to a better tomorrow.
